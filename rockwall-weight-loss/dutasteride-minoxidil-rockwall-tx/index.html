    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Rockwall, TX

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Rockwall

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil works on growth, not hormones. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. Most providers want to see about a year of consistent finasteride use with progress photos before switching, since some men simply need more time on the first drug. Fast early loss can move the timeline up. Women of childbearing potential cannot use it.

    Dosing and Schedule in Rockwall

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil is applied daily or taken orally. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. That early shed is common, and it typically settles by month three as follicles move into a new growth phase. Around month six, density often improves. In head-to-head studies, dutasteride 0.5 mg produced greater increases in hair count than finasteride 1 mg over six months, which is why it is the common step up. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Rockwall

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. The long half-life means side effects can take weeks to months to clear. Report low mood promptly. Scalp itch is the usual topical issue. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. Dutasteride lowers PSA by about half, so any doctor screening your prostate needs to know you take it and will usually double the reading to interpret it. No blood donation during use or for six months after. Pregnant partners should not touch leaking capsules.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Rockwall

Dutasteride + minoxidil is stronger but off-label for hair in the US, and its long half-life makes it slower to wash out if side effects show up. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is a topical-only choice.
